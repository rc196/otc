Our Story

Oakland Track Club is a social and competitive running club established in 2017. Our athletes compete in variety of events and distances, ranging from road racing and track, to cross country and trail ultras.

Our members span in ability from people beginning their love affair with running to seasoned veterans; we encourage everyone to come out and join us. Racing is not required.

We believe that the joy of running and racing should be accessible and welcoming to all.

Run with us

Oakland Track Club hosts regular runs in Oakland and longer runs around the East Bay and beyond, plus two annual track races: the Summer Classic and the Winter Classic, and one marathon/relay: the Lake Merrithon.
Many of our members race in local, state, national, and international races, so you'll always find encouraging training partners.
