Oakland Track Club

Oakland Track Club is a competitive and social running club established in 2017 based in the heart of the East Bay. We welcome runners of all backgrounds to join us in the life-shaping experience of setting and chasing big goals with a group of inspirational teammates.

Weekly
Runs

Saturdays | 8 AMLake LoopsLake Merritt Pergola

Our staple club run! Meet at 8:00am for a lap or two (or three) around our home 3.1 mile "track." The group always starts out easy and we generally have pace groups ranging from about 7 to 10 minutes a mile.

The pergola is at the NE corner of the lake near the Lakeview Library.

Tuesdays | 6:15 AMTemescal Tuesday40th and Telegraph (NE Corner)

This is a casual, shorter run starting in Temescal at 6:15am on Tuesday mornings. The main course is a 5.5 mile loop, and the paces are generally between 6:30 - 8:30 min/mile.

Wednesdays | 6:15 AMWorkout WednesdayWitter Track at Piedmont High School

The biggest run of the week (we are a track club after all). We meet for mid-week speed and strength workouts at 6:15am on Wednesday mornings at Witter Track in Piedmont.

Thursdays | 6:15 PMJueves de Tacos RunLos Cantaros

Paused for the winter. Will restart on January 21, 2027.

On Thursday evenings we meet at 6:15pm at Los Cantaros on Grand Avenue for an organized group run of 1-2 loops of Lake Merritt, followed by food and laughs! Spanish speakers are encouraged to join us — but all are welcome!

Fridays | 6:15 AMSunrise Tempo RunCleveland Cascade at Lake Merritt

A tempo run around the shores of Lake Merritt. Some take it easy while others bring their tempo pace!

Membership Benefits

Organized group workouts and runs

We host runs nearly every day in locations around Oakland. Joining OTC is a great way to get motivated and find runners with similar goals and paces to run with.

Discounts from our sponsors and partners

Enjoy exclusive discounts from Rabbit and club-only discount codes to a number of local and regional races, both road and trail.

Rabbit Racing Singlet & Members-only gear

New members get a Rabbit singlet so they can rep the club in style. Plus, members get access to our gear shop where we sell affordable training gear.

Training and Racing Partners

Train together for races with OTC, including local stalwarts such as the Oakland Half and Full Marathon, Napa, and CIM, and major national races such as Chicago, Boston, and NYC. Do a track or road relay race, or race as a team in Cross Country.

Vibrant Slack Community

Keep up with everything that is happening in the club and with teammates in our Slack community. Here you'll find channels about racing, food, pets, and spaces for Femmes/Women/Nonbinary runners, injured runners, and trail runners.

Choose the dues that fit your circumstances

Our suggested annual membership is $75. We ask that members contribute an amount that is meaningful to them. A guideline that we use is what you would pay for a new pair of running shoes or a dinner out for your family.

Many members find that they would like to contribute an amount between these numbers. If that's you, please choose the lower amount and then donate the rest here.

To become a member, please contact membership@oaklandtrackclub.org
