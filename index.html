Oakland Track Club is a competitive and social running club established in 2017 based in the heart of the East Bay. We welcome runners of all backgrounds to join us in the life-shaping experience of setting and chasing big goals with a group of inspirational teammates.

Join us

We offer multiple club runs to meet any schedule and most abilities. Every few months, we host a newcomers run at Lake Merritt. Join our Strava group to keep up with the run schedule.

The people are what make OTC special. Everyone genuinely supports each other, whether that’s showing up to cheer at local races or helping each other get through workouts.

There’s something motivating about training alongside people who are working toward their own goals and are just as excited to see you reach yours.
